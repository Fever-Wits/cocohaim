# 0009 — The read-through is the text; the chapters are gone

**Status:** accepted (3 Sep 2026) — supersedes
[0002](0002-chapters-are-the-source.md).

**Context:** [0002](0002-chapters-are-the-source.md) kept the chapter files
as the source and the joined file as a copy of them, until a rewrite. The
rewrite is done: [cocohaim.md](../cocohaim.md) was written anew, from the
author's telling, not joined from the chapters. Keeping the chapters beside
it would mean two texts saying one thing in two ways, and drifting.

**Decision:** [cocohaim.md](../cocohaim.md) is the text. The eight chapters
(`problem` · `mechanism` · `practices` · `collaboration` · `limits` ·
`origin` · `start-here` · `glossary`) are removed. What they said and the
read-through does not is listed in the authors' working record, to enter
when each piece has its example and its place. The shelves — `tests/`,
`patterns/`, `references.md` — stay; the read-through points to them, and
they do not retell it.

**Consequences:** one place for every claim. Links into the chapters were
redirected (the entry page, two patterns, decisions 0003, 0007, 0008).
References were pruned to the entries still cited — 38 to 10, renumbered;
earlier changelog entries use the old numbering. The changelog keeps the
chapters' names as history.

*Where it shows:* [README](../README.md) · [cocohaim.md](../cocohaim.md) ·
[references](../references.md).
