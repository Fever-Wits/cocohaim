# 0006 — The top floor is one story; the mechanism is a chapter inside it

**Status:** accepted (2 Sep 2026) — supersedes the layered map of 25 Aug
(five layers, each its own file, the entry page as the only thread).

**Context:** a method document of this genre has a short top floor read in one
pass and an atomic reference below it. Ours had the reference and no top
floor: a reader with none of our context stopped at the atoms and called the
whole thing "too atomic". The question was which carrier leads the top floor
— the story of what happened (an experience report) or the explanation of
why it works (the mechanism). Three things from the text pointed the same
way: the mechanism does not yet predict, so it cannot carry a reader alone;
the one outside reading test was carried by the story; and what is unique
here is the road, not the end-point.

**Decision:** the top floor is one artifact — the story, first person plural,
where "we" is the partnership (a human and a model; the authorship note on
the [entry page](../README.md#status) says who). The mechanism and the collaboration chapters
become sections inside it; the origin chapter dissolves into it. One worked
example — the documented case in [evidence](../evidence/README.md) — runs
through the story and through every pattern. Practices stay in the reference
floor and appear in the story where they were found.

**Consequences:** [cocohaim.md](../cocohaim.md) is rewritten, not joined;
the chapter files become reference or dissolve; the "under reorganization"
note and [0002](0002-chapters-are-the-source.md) fall when the rewrite
lands; the story owes the reader an authorship line, or "we" has no address.

*Where it shows:* not yet in the text — this record binds the rewrite.
