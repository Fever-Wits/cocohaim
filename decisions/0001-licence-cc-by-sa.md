# 0001 — Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0

**Status:** accepted (25 Aug 2026)

**Context:** the text is meant to be reused and adapted by others; changes
should stay open, and the author's name should stay on it. The Scrum Guide
(open, attribution, share-alike) was the precedent looked at.

**Decision:** CC BY-SA 4.0, © 2026 Aleksandar Hristov. The model's
contribution is declared, not licensed — a tool does not sign.

**Consequences:** anyone may copy and adapt with attribution and the same
licence; a closed fork is not allowed; the human, not the model, is
accountable for what the text says.
