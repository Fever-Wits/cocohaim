# 0007 — cocohaim is a *method*; *model* is the machine; *mechanism* is the why

**Status:** accepted (2 Sep 2026)

**Context:** one word carried three meanings in the text: the language model
(the machine the reader works with), cocohaim itself (a way of working), and
the explanation of why the way works. A reader meeting "the model" could not
tell which was meant, and the name's own expansion ended in the same word.

**Decision:** the name expands to *COgnitive COllaboration Human–AI
**Method***. In the text: *model* is reserved for the language model;
*method* names cocohaim as a way of working; *mechanism* names the
explanation of why it works (it is also the chapter's name). The glossary
carries the three, one line each.

**Consequences:** every occurrence of "model" in the second and third sense
is renamed; sentences that contrasted "the model and the method" are
rewritten as "the method and the mechanism behind it"; the name stays as it
is — only its last letter changes what it stands for.

*Where it shows:* the first line of [README](../README.md). It also showed
in `glossary.md` and under the heading "What this mechanism does not claim"
in `mechanism.md`, until both were folded into
[cocohaim.md](../cocohaim.md) in September 2026.
